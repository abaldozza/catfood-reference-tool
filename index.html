Ca:P Ratio Guidance & Sources

Ca:P ratio guidance is based on AAFCO/FEDIAF adult maintenance recommendations and feline feeding studies. Ranges shown reflect adult maintenance guidance — kitten/growth foods have a narrower recommended range (1:1–1.5:1 per FEDIAF).

  • Laflamme D, et al. A review of phosphorus homeostasis and the impact of different types and amounts of dietary phosphate on metabolism and renal health in cats. J Vet Intern Med. 2020;34(6):2187-2196.
  • Coltherd JC, et al. Dietary calcium to phosphorus ratio affects postprandial phosphorus concentrations in feline plasma. Br J Nutr. 2022;128(9):1689-1699.
  • FEDIAF Nutritional Guidelines for Complete and Complementary Pet Food for Cats and Dogs.
  • AAFCO Cat Food Nutrient Profiles.
Important: This tool is provided for educational and reference purposes only and is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice. Calculations are estimates based on guaranteed-analysis label values, not laboratory assay results, and nutrient needs — including calcium, phosphorus, and their ratio — vary with a cat's health status, life stage, body condition, and activity level. Ca:P ranges shown reflect adult maintenance guidance and do not account for kittens, pregnant/nursing cats, or cats on a therapeutic diet, who may need different ratios. Always consult your veterinarian before making changes to your cat's diet, especially if your cat has an existing health condition.
